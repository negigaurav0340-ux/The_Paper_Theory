Sticker sheet sizes, finish and delivery

Which regular sheet should I choose?

Choose 16 one-inch designs in a 4 × 4 grid, 12 two-inch designs in a 3 × 4 grid, or 6 three-inch designs in a 2 × 3 grid. Every regular laminated sheet is ₹99; holographic sheets are ₹199.

Can I mix long sticker formats?

Large sheets contain four 6 × 2-inch stickers. Extra Large sheets contain two 10 × 2-inch stickers. Each ₹99 long sheet uses the complete matching pack.

Where can I apply them?

Apply laminated vinyl stickers to clean, dry, smooth surfaces such as laptop lids and notebooks. Avoid textured surfaces, abrasive cleaning and high heat.

How does delivery work?

Dispatch usually takes 2–4 business days. Courier timing and shipping are confirmed for your delivery PIN code; product subtotals of ₹699 or more qualify for free shipping.

We use anonymous usage events and approximate location to improve the shop. Delivery-location reports contain only city, state and country; names, phone numbers and street addresses are excluded.
