YOUR NEXT HAPPY MAIL

A simple way to order.

01

Choose your design

Pick a complete pack, build a sticker sheet, or choose a Light Box. Regular sheets are ₹99; holographic sheets are ₹199.

02

Check your bag

Choose the finish and quantity. Use minus to remove an item at zero. Polaroid photo selection opens when you start an order.

03

Add your delivery details

Select your country and state, then fill your address. Review everything before sharing it.

04

Confirm on WhatsApp

Your order message includes the items and delivery address. We confirm shipping, artwork and payment instructions in the conversation. The website does not take payment or automatically place an order.

Ordering photo gifts?

Share a Google Drive folder with viewer access, or select photos for a local preview and send the original files on WhatsApp. Packs of 6 and 12 need the matching photo count. Selecting photos does not upload them.

Shipping and dispatch

Dispatch usually takes 2–4 business days. Domestic product subtotals of ₹699 or more qualify for free shipping. International availability and shipping are confirmed separately before payment.

After you receive your order

Request tracking in your order conversation. For damage, send your order reference and clear packaging and product photos so we can review it. Leave an anonymous review on the product page after trying your purchase.
